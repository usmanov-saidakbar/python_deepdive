Python Deep Dive Spark Roadmap

2-Week Spark Roadmap

10 study days, Monday to Friday, about 5 focused hours each. Every day has three parts: Theory, Lab and Recall. Tick off each item as you go; your notes are saved with your checklist.

Overall progress
Checklist coverage

Every question on the module checklist, the day that covers it, and whether its answer card is ticked. Use it to pick questions for the Day 10 mock checkpoint.

How a day works
PartTimeWhat you do
1. Theory
Learn the answers		≈ 1 h 45 minRead the linked sources (each says which sections and how long), then write one answer card per checklist question. The points after Cover: are what a complete answer contains.
🧭 How Spark decidesExtra cards on Days 1–3 and 10 that go one level deeper: how Spark plans, schedules and re-plans your query.
2. Lab
Prove it in PySpark and the Spark UI		≈ 2 h 30 minOne lab built on the starter kit lab_utils.py. Each task says what to run, what you should see, and which questions it proves. The last task breaks something on purpose.
3. Recall
Say it without notes		≈ 40 minAnswer today's cards aloud, re-answer the ones you marked on earlier days, and pass the Done when test.

Setup: Python 3.10 or newer (3.12 recommended), Java 17 or 21, then pip install pyspark==4.2.0 delta-spark==4.4.1 "pyarrow>=18" "pandas>=2.2,<3". Day 1 walks you through it.

Habits: predict the plan before you run it, then check it with explain('formatted'). Keep the Spark UI open at localhost:4040. Paste the plans you save into your Lab notes.

Short day? Do the Theory and the first half of the Lab, and move the rest to the weekend. Do not skip Recall: it is what makes the answers stick.
