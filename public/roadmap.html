15-Day Spark Mastery Roadmap

Every day has three parts: Theory, Lab and Exercise. Tick off each item as you go. Your notes are saved with your checklist.

In the Lab and Exercise parts, each activity opens with a What to learn first list of the commands, settings, UI screens and functions you need, followed by the tasks under Then do.

Habits: predict the plan, then check it with df.explain('formatted'). Keep the Spark UI open at localhost:4040. Write a one-page log every day.

Short day? Do the Theory, the guided lab and the self-test, and move the rest to the next afternoon. Never skip the Lab or the drills.
