Python Deep Dive Spark Roadmap

2-Week Spark Roadmap

10 study days, Monday to Friday, about 4 focused hours each. Every day has two parts: Theory and a Lab notebook. Tick off each item as you go; your notes are saved with your checklist.

Overall progress
Checklist coverage

Every question on the module checklist, the day that covers it, and whether its answer card is ticked. Use it to pick questions for the Day 10 mock checkpoint.

How a day works
PartTimeWhat you do
1. Theory
Learn the answers		≈ 1 h 45 minRead the linked sources (each says which sections and how long), then write one answer card per checklist question. The points after Cover: are what a complete answer contains. Cards in red are the checklist questions you will be asked; go deeper on them if needed.
🧭 How Spark decidesExtra cards on Days 1–3 and 10 that go one level deeper: how Spark plans, schedules and re-plans your query.
2. Lab
Solve the lab notebook		≈ 2 h 30 minDownload the day's .ipynb notebook and lab_utils.py, open it in Jupyter, and solve every task in the empty cell under it: code along with an official tutorial or GitHub example, two exercises with a Check and a reference solution, and short checks in the plan and the Spark UI.

Setup: Python 3.10 or newer (3.12 recommended), Java 17 or 21, then pip install pyspark==4.2.0 delta-spark==4.4.1 "pyarrow>=18" "pandas>=2.2,<3" jupyterlab pytest memory-profiler. Day 1 walks you through it.

Habits: predict the plan before you run it, then check it with explain('formatted'). Keep the Spark UI open at localhost:4040. Paste the plans you save into your Lab notes.

Short day? Do the Theory and the first half of the Lab, and move the rest to the weekend. Before the next day, answer yesterday's cards aloud without notes.
