Python Deep Dive Spark Roadmap

2-Week Spark Roadmap

10 study days, Monday to Friday, about 4 focused hours each. Every day has two parts: Theory and a Lab notebook. Tick off each item as you go; your notes are saved with your checklist.

Overall progress
Checklist coverage

Every question on the module checklist, the day that covers it, and whether its topic is ticked. Use it to pick questions for the Day 10 mock checkpoint.

How a day works
PartTimeWhat you do
1. Theory
Learn the checklist topics		≈ 1 h 15 min3–4 topics a day. Each one shows the checklist questions it answers (in red), one article to read, and one sentence on what to learn. Write your answers to the questions in your own words, then check them against the article. Topics marked beyond the checklist go one level deeper.
2. Lab
Solve the lab notebook		≈ 2 h 30 minDownload the day's .ipynb notebook, open it in Jupyter and answer every question in the empty cell under it. Every lab works on the same single table of online-shop orders, which the notebook creates for you. Each question has a check cell (✅ or ❌), the documentation list at the top tells you where to research, and each lab ends with DataFrame coding practice. Day 10 is a final project built from scratch, plus a timed practice set.

Setup (once): install Java 17 or 21 (java -version to check) and Python 3.10 or newer (3.12 recommended; macOS ships 3.9, which is too old). Then in a new folder: python3.12 -m venv .venv, source .venv/bin/activate, pip install pyspark==4.2.0 delta-spark==4.4.1 "pyarrow>=18" "pandas>=2.2,<3" jupyterlab, then jupyter lab. The first notebook that uses Delta downloads a jar, which takes a minute or two.

Habits: predict the plan before you run it, then check it with explain('formatted'). Keep the Spark UI open at localhost:4040. Write what you learn in your Theory notes.

Short day? Do the Theory and the first half of the Lab, and finish the rest at the weekend.
