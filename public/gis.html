Overall progress
How to use this roadmap

Goal: in 12 months, solidify GIS fundamentals, reach the level of a GeoAI (AI + spatial data) specialist, and build urban analytics knowledge in parallel. Time split: GIS fundamentals ~25%, GeoAI ~55%, urban analytics ~20%. Stage 2 (spatial data science) is the bridge: all later GeoAI work rests on it. Shift the months if you need to, but keep the order of the stages.

The 4-step loop for every topic (top-down):

StepWhat you do
UseGet a result with a library or QGIS (1–2 days).
BreakChange parameters, the CRS, the data size; write down what changes in the stage's Break log.
UnderstandNow read the theory and the formula; your questions are already waiting.
RebuildWrite a small version from scratch with numpy or shapely (50–100 lines), or explain it to someone in 5 sentences.

Depth levels (the badge next to each topic decides which steps it needs):

LevelMeaning"Done" when
L2 UserYou get correct results with the toolYou used it on real data without errors: Use
L3 UnderstanderYou know why it works and when it breaksUse, Break, Understand, with entries in your Break log
L4 BuilderYou know it down to the formula and can write it from scratchAll four steps: your from-scratch version matches the library

L1 (aware: you can explain it in 2–3 sentences) is the starting point for everything. L4 only for the GeoAI core (spatial statistics, spatial CV, segmentation, GNNs); L2–L3 is enough everywhere else: don't dive into the math of everything.

Weekly rhythm (~10–12 hours):

When to stop: an L2 topic once you used it and got a correct result; L3 when you can explain why it works and when it breaks; L4 when your from-scratch version matches the library's result. If a topic took twice the planned time, flag it and move on: you'll return in the next round.

Two risks of the top-down method: staying stuck in the Use step and becoming a button-pusher, or the opposite, sinking into one formula and building nothing for weeks. The weekly check catches both.

Quarterly review: every 3 months, redo the Stage 0 self-assessment and adjust this plan to your real pace.

Weekly check

Sunday, 20 minutes. Answer the four questions for this week:

  • What did I build this week? (If nothing, cut back theory next week.)
  • Which topic reached L3 or L4? (If none, you skipped the Understand step.)
  • How many entries did my Break log get?
  • Which other subject or course connected to this week's work?
